Data Inferences

Accelerating decline

Genuine AI-citation volume has now dropped for 3 straight weeks, and the drop is accelerating. Ex-Meta AI events ran 17,539 → 14,235 → 4,969 → 3,849 (+8.8%, −18.8%, −65.1%, −22.5% week-on-week) — a cumulative 78% fall across this 4-week window alone, confirming and sharpening the quarter-long decline shown in the Executive Insights chart above. This isn't a one-week blip; it's the same trend continuing to worsen.

Rising overcount

Meta AI's share of AI-agent reads is climbing every single week. 63.2% → 71.4% → 78.2% → 79.0% over these 4 weeks (and 59.4% the week before that). The more this share grows, the less the headline "96–97% AI-agent share" figure tells you about real citation activity — by Sep 2–9, roughly 4 in 5 AI-agent reads were Meta's own link-preview fetch, not a model actually retrieving the docs.

Data-quality flag

The Aug 26–Sep 2 search spike (719 vs. a normal 20–85/week) is not real search demand. 611 of those 719 searches were the single character "c," repeated — almost certainly an automated or scripted query, not organic users. Left uncorrected, this would make that week look like a 10–30x jump in content-gap signal; it isn't one.

Sticky content

The homepage dominates every week by a wide margin — "Welcome to Filecoin Docs" pulled 459, 612, 540, and 447 page views across the 4 weeks, 3–4x the #2 page each time. "What is Filecoin," "Install & Run PDP," "Getting started," and "Crypto-economics" round out the top ranks consistently; nothing new broke in.

Stable leader

The United States has led every one of these 4 weeks on unique visitors (800, 767, 899, 620), with Singapore a consistent, distant second (334, 356, 317, 312). Worth noting this is a reversal from the pattern seen earlier in the quarter (see the 3-month country split above, where the two are closer at the aggregate level) — the leader isn't fixed over longer windows, just currently stable week to week.

Early, still tiny

MCP-protocol reads are nonzero but negligible — 8, 1, 1, and 0 calls across the 4 weeks, against 3,000+ page views each week. Not material yet, but worth tracking as a leading indicator of agentic/tool-based doc consumption as MCP adoption grows elsewhere.

"Genuine AI-citation events (ex-Meta AI)" = the sum of named LLM/AI-agent rows (Meta AI, ChatGPT, OpenAI, Perplexity, and similar) excluding Meta AI itself, "Unknown," and "Unidentified." Meta AI is excluded because its volume is overwhelmingly automated re-fetching of /images/social-card.png for link previews (typically 60–90%+ of its own total) rather than the docs actually being read or cited by a model. "Unknown" and "Unidentified" are excluded because they can't be attributed to a specific AI agent, model, or crawler.
