Treat this week's uptick in genuine citations as tentative, not a confirmed reversal — one week isn't a trend.

Data Inferences

Tentative reversal

Genuine AI-citation volume rose for the first time in a month. Ex-Meta AI events ran 14,235 → 4,969 → 3,849 → 4,405 (−18.8%, −65.1%, −22.5%, then +14.4% week-on-week) — the first week-on-week increase since the Week 2 quarter peak (33,769), but still the second-lowest of these 4 weeks and 87% below that peak. One week of upward movement doesn't confirm a turnaround; worth watching whether next week's pull holds or reverts.

Meta AI share hit a new high

Meta AI's share of AI-agent reads climbed again this week, to 80.4%. 71.4% → 78.2% → 79.0% → 80.4% over these 4 weeks. By this week, roughly 4 in 5 AI-agent reads were Meta's own link-preview fetch of /images/social-card.png, not a model actually retrieving the docs — even as the raw ex-Meta citation count ticked up, it's a shrinking share of an already-shrunken metric.

Visitors stabilized, not recovered

Unique visitors rose 2.6% week-on-week after last week's sharp 17.5% drop, but at 1,537 remain well below the quarter's early-June level (index 70 vs. Week 1's 100) — a much milder decline than the AI-citation series over the same window, but a decline all the same.

Narrower geographic reach

The trailing 30-day window reached only 43 distinct countries, down from 62 in the prior 30-day snapshot (Aug 10–Sep 9), even as headline page views and visitors held roughly flat. That points to a thinner long tail of one-off international visits rather than a broader base eroding.

GitBook restructured Analytics this pull

The old Visitor Type + Bots + MCP layout is now three tabs — Traffic, Agents, AI assistant — and the new "Agents" list mixes AI/LLM agents (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini…) with classic search/index crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot…) in one flat, unfiltered list. I kept the same "genuine AI-citation" definition by judgment call — excluding Meta AI, Unknown, Unidentified, and classic crawlers — but the exact category boundaries GitBook uses internally may differ; worth a gut-check next week if the trend looks off.

MCP still negligible

MCP-protocol reads remain near-zero — 1, 1, 0, and 0 calls across these 4 weeks, against thousands of page views each week. Not material yet, but still worth tracking as adoption of agentic/tool-based doc consumption grows elsewhere.

"Genuine AI-citation events (ex-Meta AI)" = the sum of named LLM/AI-agent rows (ChatGPT, OpenAI, Perplexity, Claude / Claude Code / Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, GitHub Copilot, Cohere, Cursor, YouBot, DuckAssist, Alexa) excluding Meta AI itself, "Unknown," and "Unidentified." Meta AI is excluded because its volume is overwhelmingly automated re-fetching of /images/social-card.png for link previews (71–80%+ of its own weekly total this period) rather than the docs actually being read or cited by a model. "Unknown" and "Unidentified" are excluded because they can't be attributed to a specific AI agent, model, or crawler. Classic search/index and training crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot, Applebot, Amazonbot, ByteSpider, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Meta web indexer, Amazon search, CCBot, DuckDuckBot) are also excluded, consistent with the prior pull's methodology — even though GitBook's current Analytics UI (redesigned since the last pull) now lists them alongside AI agents in a single "Agents" breakdown rather than a separate crawler/bot section.
