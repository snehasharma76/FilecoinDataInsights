No Perplexity- or Claude-referred visits this month, despite Perplexity appearing on GitBook's crawler-side Agent & LLMs list — "which AI is most active" ranks differently depending on whether you're counting retrieval bots (GitBook) or referred human clicks (Plausible); the two rankings don't merge into one list. Entry pages for this segment skew toward deep technical/developer content (onchain cloud, cookbook: store data, ERC-20 quickstart, Filecoin and IPFS, crypto-economics, the FIL token) rather than the homepage-heavy pattern of overall traffic — a content-strategy signal GitBook's page totals don't split out by traffic source at all.
Don't merge these two numbers: GitBook's "genuine AI-citation events" (thousands per week) counts automated crawler/agent fetches of the docs — the AI system itself retrieving pages. Plausible's "AI Assistants" channel (23 visitors this month) counts human visitors who clicked a link after getting an AI-generated answer. They're complementary signals about two different things — retrieval volume vs. referred human engagement — never a single combined metric. Sample size caveat: n=23 visitors for the whole month — directionally interesting, not statistically robust yet. Worth re-pulling this filtered view monthly to see if the engagement gap and entry-page pattern hold up.

Data Inferences

Meta AI's share collapsed this week

Meta AI's share of AI-agent reads dropped from 80.4% to 53.7% — the sharpest one-week swing in this trend. Its raw request count fell from 67,056 to 24,349 (−63.7%), while "Unknown" and "Unidentified" rows grew to roughly a third of all AI-agent reads this week (vs. a much smaller share in prior weeks). That pattern looks at least partly like a classification shift on GitBook's side rather than a pure drop in Meta AI's own crawling — worth confirming with next week's pull before reading too much into it.

Methodology correction: genuine AI-citation

This report now follows the runbook's literal definition exactly. The Sep 9 and Sep 16 reports excluded Amazonbot and ByteSpider from "genuine AI-citation" by treating them as classic crawlers — but the runbook actually lists them as named AI agents alongside ChatGPT and Perplexity. This week's figures (and the 30-day/3-month rollups) include them; the two prior weeks' figures in the trend table above do not. The gap is modest in dollar terms (a few hundred to a few thousand requests per week) but is a real definitional break at Week 13.

Visitors have stabilized

Unique visitors have held in a tight 1,498–1,546 band for three straight weeks (index 76 → 78 → 79 off the new quarter base), a much calmer pattern than the AI-citation and AI-agent-volume series over the same stretch. Page views this week (3,737) were the highest of the current 4-week window.

Total AI-agent volume fell sharply

Total AI-agent reads dropped 45.6% week-on-week (83,361 → 45,347), the steepest single-week decline in this trend — driven mostly by the Meta AI drop above rather than a broad-based pullback across all named agents (ChatGPT actually held roughly flat at 2,621 vs. recent weeks). Pair with the classification-shift note above when assessing whether this is durable.

Search queries look organic again

This week's 60 searches (x402, Filecoin Pay, sector, aurora, ledger, storage proofs) read as real developer queries with no repeated-string pattern — a contrast with the Aug 26–Sep 2 week, whose single-character spam spike ("c," 611 hits) still dominates the rolling 30-day and 3-month search totals shown elsewhere in this report.

Geographic reach narrower again

The trailing 30-day window reached only 15 distinct countries, continuing the narrowing pattern flagged in recent reports (62 → 43 → 15 across the last three 30-day snapshots). Core traffic (page views, visitors) isn't eroding at anywhere near this rate, so this looks like a thinning long tail of one-off international visits rather than a broader base declining.

"Genuine AI-citation events (ex-Meta AI)" = the sum of named LLM/AI-agent rows (ChatGPT, OpenAI, Perplexity, Amazonbot, ByteSpider, and other named agents) excluding Meta AI itself, "Unknown," and "Unidentified." Meta AI is excluded because the large majority of its volume is automated re-fetching of /images/social-card.png for link previews, not the docs actually being read or cited by a model. Each pull samples only the first page of GitBook's paginated Agents table (top ~7 rows) rather than fully paginating — spot-checked coverage is 96–99% of that week's AI-agent volume, an acceptable approximation. Plausible cross-check added this week: a second, independent measurement source (public dashboard, no login) confirming top-pages agreement and surfacing an engagement-quality signal (AI-referred visitor segment) GitBook cannot produce on its own.
